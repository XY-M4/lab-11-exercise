2b. Sprite depth can be implemented using OpenGL's depth buffer by enabling `GL_DEPTH_TEST` and assigning different Z coordinates to sprites in the vertex shader. The GPU automatically compares fragment depths and determines which sprite is closer to the camera, allowing sprites to overlap correctly without manually sorting them.
